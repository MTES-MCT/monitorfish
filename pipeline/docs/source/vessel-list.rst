===========
Vessel list
===========

The vessel list (*"Liste des navires"*), opened in the side window, is a table of all vessels known to Monitorfish - 
whether they emit VMS or not - with their last position, fleet segments, gears, species onboard, last controls...

Filters
-------

Vessels can be filtered on :

* nationality, maritime district and length
* :doc:`risk factor <risk-factor>` (super users)
* :doc:`fleet segments <fleet-segments>`, gears and species onboard
* date of the last sea control and of the last land control
* landing ports (including ports of manual prior notifications)
* producer organization membership
* zones : administrative zones, regulatory zones, or zones drawn on the map (several zones can be combined)
* date of the last VMS position
* VMS and logbook equipment, and whether the vessel is at sea or in port

The filtered vessels are previewed on the map.

Actions
-------

* select vessels and export them as a CSV file
* create a :doc:`group of vessels <groups-of-vessels>` from the current filters (dynamic group) or from the selected vessels (fixed group)
* center the map on a vessel and open its :doc:`vessel sheet <vessel-sheet>`
